MyDocs Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · App: MyDocs (package com.alichade.mydocs) · Developer: Ali Chade

MyDocs is a document manager that keeps your documents on your own phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your documents stay on your device, the app has no ads and no analytics, and we do not collect your documents.

1. What stays on your device

  • Your documents (PDF, images, Word, text), their names, categories and dates are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Your settings (theme, language, document size limit, App Lock choice) and your plan status are stored on your phone.
  • We do not receive, view or store any of this on our servers.

2. Permissions the app uses

Permission / featureWhy
Camera (optional)To capture a document photo when you choose "Capture". The photo is saved only inside the app.
Files / document pickerTo let you choose files you want to add, or a backup file to restore. The app only receives the files you pick.
Biometric / screen lock (optional)For the App Lock feature. Authentication is done by Android; the app never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN.
InternetFor Google Play purchases, promo codes and the optional Google Drive backup.
BillingTo let you buy MyDocs Pro through Google Play.

3. Information that leaves your device

  • Google Play purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play which purchases your Google account owns so that Pro can be unlocked and restored.
  • Promo codes. When you redeem a promo code, the app sends the code and a random, app-specific device identifier to our server so that the code can be checked and used only once. No name, email, phone number or documents are sent.
  • Google Drive backup (optional). If you choose "Upload to Google Drive", you sign in with your Google account and the app uploads the backup file to a "MyDocs Backups" folder in your own Google Drive. The app asks only for the limited drive.file permission, which lets it see just the files it created. The file goes directly from your phone to your Drive; we do not receive it. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Drive.
  • Sharing. When you share a document or a ZIP, it is handed to the app you choose in Android's share sheet. What that app does with it is governed by its own policy.

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no analytics or tracking, no crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not read your documents.

5. Backups are not encrypted

A MyDocs backup file (.mydocsbackup) contains readable copies of your documents. Keep it somewhere safe and share it only with people you trust. Anyone who has the file can open it.

6. Retention and deletion

  • You can delete any document inside the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its documents and settings from your phone. Backups you saved to your device or to Google Drive are separate files and stay until you delete them.
  • Promo-code redemption records on our server contain only the code's hashed form and the app-specific device identifier. To have a record removed, contact us at the email below.

7. Security

Documents are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a normal Android phone. You can add App Lock to require your screen lock to open MyDocs. Network requests use HTTPS. No method of storage is perfectly secure, and a rooted or compromised phone can expose app data.

8. Children

MyDocs is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
